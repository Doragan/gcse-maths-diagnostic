# Question-Bank vs 2024 Exams — Workstream ⑧ (Exam-Weighted Coverage)

_Read-only cross-reference of the published bank (133 questions, 113 skills)
against all 12 coded 2024 papers (`data/exam-audit/`, 444 part-rows).
Marks are **involvement-weighted**: a 3-mark part listing two skills credits 3 to
each. Date: 2026-06-10._

**Cross-validation:** every `skill_id` used across all 444 exam rows exists in the
skill graph — zero coding typos. The exam audit and the graph agree.

## Headline
- **33 exam-tested skills have ZERO published questions** — together they touch
  ~**280 marks** across the 2024 series.
- Of the 38 question-less skills found in workstream ②, **33 appeared in 2024
  exams** — almost the entire coverage gap is exam-relevant, not theoretical.
- Several of the **heaviest-traffic skills have only 1 question**: a single
  parametric template can't sustain varied practice toward the 5-attempt mastery
  window.
